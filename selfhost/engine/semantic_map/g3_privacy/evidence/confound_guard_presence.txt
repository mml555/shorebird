THE CONFOUND, as G0 recorded it (id PLATFORM_LIBRARY_GUARD_IS_UNCOMMITTED):
the guard refusing --resolve-private-names-in-library on a platform library
is uncommitted-but-shipped source, present in effective tree 7b04b01b and
absent from a clean checkout of 9e8c898a. Built from the wrong tree, the
platform-library arm passes vacuously -- 'nothing was granted' looks exactly
like 'the guard never ran'.

METHOD. The shared engine checkout is NEVER mutated. The dart tree's
package_config uses RELATIVE rootUris (../pkg/front_end), so an APFS
clonefile copy resolves its OWN CFE. The clone (3.2G, 41s, near-zero bytes)
has only the isScheme('dart') refusal removed: 0005 without its fix.

THE PROBE BODY reaches dart:core's private namespace and nothing else
private. An earlier version also read the app's own private member, and
that made arm 3a pass for the wrong reason: under flag=dart:core the app
namespace is out of scope, so the body failed on the APP private whether or
not the guard existed. Narrowed to a platform private alone, a guard-less
tree COMPILES it -- which is precisely the hole P1.1 arm A5 found.
