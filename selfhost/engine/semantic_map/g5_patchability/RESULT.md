## Disposition

### REDUCE_SCOPE

Two things are required, and both are proven above: nothing is admitted on
any corpus, AND both scope reasons are present on every replaceable
declaration of every corpus. Zero-admitted alone would not justify this —
that could be an accident of the corpus rather than a scope reduction.

Zero replaceable declarations are admitted on any corpus. The subset
property `predicted ⊆ demonstrated` holds everywhere it was checked, and
holds **vacuously** on all three corpora — which is recorded as a distinct
verdict (`SUBSET_HOLDS_VACUOUSLY`) precisely so it cannot be read as
evidence that anything is patchable.

**Coverage was not used as a safety gate.** It is reported as a diagnostic
and never gates publication; the verdict above would be identical at any
coverage figure, and a single admitted-but-undemonstrated declaration would
make it `FAIL_OPEN` regardless of how high coverage were.

## What is NOT established

* That any declaration is safely patchable. Nothing is admitted.
* That `NOT_INLINED` implies a patch takes effect. `Base.work` is
  `NOT_INLINED`, attaches, invokes as patched — and an ordinary polymorphic
  call site still reads the old value.
* That a pool-indirect call shape is redirectable. `UntaggedFunction` and
  `UntaggedCode` entry-point offsets alias, so the redirectable and
  non-redirectable forms are byte-identical in the shipped text.
* That front-end materialization is witnessed by any VM-level evidence.
* Behavioural patchability on the app corpora: not measured, and not
  required for an empty prediction set.
