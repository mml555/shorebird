############ WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES ############
  1. The demonstrator is now FAITHFUL and has a trusted positive control.
     DEMONSTRATOR_FIDELITY=ESTABLISHED

  2. --patchable_static_calls is a RELEASE-BUILD PRECONDITION for
     patchability. It is not a property of the declaration, the map, or the
     patch; it is how the release binary was produced.

  3. Its absence FAILS OPEN. The container verified hashes, matched the
     release id, attached the bytecode, and reported 'APPLY ok: 1 target(s)'

     silent-bypass shape SM1-G4 measured for two retention classes, now
     observed at the apply layer.
