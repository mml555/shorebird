THE PROBE, AND WHY IT CANNOT BE VACUOUS
----------------------------------------
Placed inside `_runMain`'s closure in `R3`'s `lib/ui/hooks.dart`:

    if (args.length == 0x7ffffffe) {
      throw StateError('G15_HOOKS_DELIVERY_MARKER_7f3a9c21');
    }

`args` is a runtime `List<String>`, so the comparison cannot be constant-folded
and the throw cannot be proved dead — which is what KEEPS the string literal in
the snapshot. Retention by construction is the whole point: with an ordinary
unreferenced constant, an ABSENT marker could not be told from tree-shaking, and
a check whose failure looks identical to its success measures nothing. A `List`
can never have that length, so the throw never executes.

**That is the anti-vacuity proof.** The marker's presence is caused by the dill

control did not simply fail to include `dart:ui`.

A SECOND RESULT, FREE, AND IT NARROWS AN OPEN QUESTION
-------------------------------------------------------
`gate3a_platform_dill_repair.txt` left open whether `dart-sdk-darwin-arm64.zip`
must move to `R3` alongside the dill, because `publish_ios_overlay.sh` asserts a
version-lock chain (`frontend_server_aot` ← `dartaotruntime` ← platform dill) and
concludes "all three come from our tree or none of this is ours".

**R3's dill paired with the cached `40eaa0ef` toolchain AOTs cleanly.** Exit 0,
`Built build/ios/iphoneos/Runner.app (24.2MB)`. No "Unexpected tag 4 (Field)", no
"Wrong full snapshot version". So the invariant is at least partly defensive: the
dill can move on its own without the dart-sdk following it.

This is NOT a licence to skip the question in general — it is one pairing
(`R3` dill + `40eaa0ef` gen_snapshot/frontend), measured, on a trivial app. It
does say the publication repair can be attempted as a one-variable change and
observed, rather than requiring the whole host toolchain to move first.
