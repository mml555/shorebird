THE LEADING CANDIDATE FOR THE CONFOUND, AND IT WAS DOCUMENTED IN ADVANCE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
**The launch method.** `arm2_verdict.txt` has a section titled WHY BY HAND:

    "There is no automated launch path here. `ios-deploy --justlaunch` quits the
     app when lldb detaches, and an attached `--debug` launch holds it at the
     launch screen with the debugger intercepting signals — unusable for an arm
     whose mechanism IS a signal."

Every one of today's four behavioral runs was launched with **`idevicedebug run`,
which attaches a debugserver.** The 2026-08-14 `NEW-kill` observations were
by-hand taps with no debugger.

A debugger-attached launch is a plausible cause of a patched `Function` not taking
effect — it changes process startup, and Route B's mechanism is an entry-point
redirection into an interpreter. **This is a hypothesis, not a finding.** It is
named because it is the difference the prior evidence explicitly warned about, and
because I read that warning, applied it to `--justlaunch`, and did not apply it to
`idevicedebug`.

Other unexcluded differences, none of them measured:

* the hybrid assembly and my re-signing (vs the shipped, originally-signed app);
* the updater-state reset and forced render arm (vs organic alternation);
* three days of device/OS state.
