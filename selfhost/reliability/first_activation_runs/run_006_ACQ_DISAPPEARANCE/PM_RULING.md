# run_006 and the unobserved 01:39:51 launch — neither counts in a controlled population

PM ruling.

**run_006** — classified **G** (orderly system/lifecycle termination:
`UIAPP_WILL_TERMINATE` present, preceded by `MEMORY_PRESSURE`). It is **not** a
reproduction of the historical phenomenon: different launch type (acquisition of an
established patch, not first activation of a new one), a positively observed
termination callback, and memory pressure twice. Logged as a G-classified
incidental.

**The unobserved 01:39:51 launch** (pid 50736) — counts in **neither** population.
It ran patch 4, downloaded patch 5, and terminated orderly with
`UIAPP_WILL_TERMINATE` at 01:43:43. Useful operational evidence, and the second
consecutive orderly termination on this device, but it was not an armed controlled
observation. Voiding the mislabelled acquisition run was correct.

## Three categories, kept separate — do not pool

    controlled A/B observations     the reproduction sample
    unarmed / incidental            run_006, the 01:39:51 launch
    historical                      the 3-for-3 from Arm C and the Signing lane

observation quality differs, and this remains a reproduction/classification
